# Spanish dictionary

Spanish definitions and word-form relationships are adapted from English
Wiktionary contributors, extracted by Wiktextract and distributed by Kaikki:
https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Spanish/

Source: https://kaikki.org/dictionary/Spanish/kaikki.org-dictionary-Spanish.jsonl.gz
Wiktionary dump: 2026-10-01. Extraction: 2026-10-30.

4.0 International: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/
Wiktionary copyright information: https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Copyrights
Entry pages and contributor histories are available at
https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/WORD#Spanish (replace WORD with the headword).
MochiRead provides an entry source link in Spanish word popups.

Changes: retain Spanish headwords, English glosses, inflection/alternative-form
links and listed word forms; normalize lookup keys to lowercase NFC; merge
homographs; omit etymology, quotations, audio and unrelated metadata; compress
as JSON rows. Definitions are not limited to a curated vocabulary or word-count
cap. Coverage and accuracy depend on Wiktionary; missing entries are possible.

Rebuild the committed compact dataset:

    python3 packages/dict/scripts/build-spanish.py /path/to/source.jsonl.gz

The compressed dataset envelope records the SHA-256 of the downloaded source.
